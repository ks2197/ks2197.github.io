Projects
Deep Reinforcement Learning for Smartgrid Control

I worked under the supervision of Prof. Andreas J. Kassler at the Intelligent Networks and Systems Lab, Deggendorf Institute of Technology, Germany. My work focused on developing and optimizing energy-sharing models using multi-agent reinforcement learning (MARL) to enhance smart grid efficiency. I designed advanced reward functions in a MADDPG environment to improve V2G and G2V energy flows, enabling smarter energy distribution between buildings and electric vehicles.

Alongside reinforcement learning–based optimization, I developed community-driven energy-sharing metrics to strengthen local energy resilience, reduce dependence on the main grid, and promote sustainable resource utilization.

To validate these models, I conducted simulations in real-world-inspired environments using Python-based frameworks such as CityLearn and custom MARL agents. These experiments demonstrated measurable improvements in energy utilization, cost reduction, and grid load balancing.

GW Skymap

I worked with the Mathematical Physics of Gravitational Waves research group at Western University, Ontario, Canada, under the supervision of Prof. Shree Ram Valluri. My work focused on developing an interactive gravitational wave visualization tool by building a GUI that integrated with the LIGO GraceDB REST API, enabling exploration of thousands of sky maps in single-order and multi-order formats using HEALPix.

I implemented 2D projected contour plots and 3D scatter plots to visualize gravitational wave source locations detected by LIGO, Virgo, and KAGRA, providing an intuitive view of event distributions.

I ensured software robustness and cross-platform compatibility by using HEALPix over HEALPy and integrated the tool into the open-source Stellarium planetarium software, making gravitational wave data accessible to the scientific community and enthusiasts.
